Wellfleet Drum and Dance 2026
A community rhythm celebration
Wellfleet Adult Community Center
715 Old Kings Hwy, Wellfleet, MA 02667
SATURDAY, November 14, 2027 7pm to 10pm

Celebrate an evening with drumming and dancing! Wellfleet Drum and Dance is about listening to each other, co-creating music and dancing our spirit.

Wellfleet Drum and Dance will strive to capture the spirit and energy of Cambridge Drum and Dance, Concord Drum and Dance and the Acton Drum and Dance Fire Circle.

We welcome drummers, dancers, and rhythm/flow artists of all kinds.

We'll dance and play to a mix of traditional West African rhythms and organized rhythm jams, with a focus on the dancers. You're welcome to bring your own hand drums and percussion, flow toys, or simply come to dance and enjoy. While many of us have studied drumming for years, others are new to the drum. We all can learn from each other as we create magical, danceable rhythms.

The event is free, family friendly, and is open to all ages and experience levels. Bring music from your heart and a sharing spirit. Please bring water to drink. No alcohol please. Chairs will be provided for all.
