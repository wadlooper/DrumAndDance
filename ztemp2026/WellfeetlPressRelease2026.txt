Wellfleet Drum and Dance 2026
A community rhythm celebration
Wellfleet Adult Community Center
715 Old Kings Hwy, Wellfleet, MA 02667
SATURDAY, November 14, 2026 7pm to 10pm

Celebrate an evening with drumming and dancing!  Wellfleet Drum and Dance is about listening to each other, co-creating music and dancing your spirit.  Drummers, dancers, and rhythm/flow artists of all kinds are welcome.

Dance and play to a mix of traditional West African rhythms and organized rhythm jams, with a focus on the dancers. You're welcome to bring your own hand drums and percussion, flow toys, or simply come to dance and enjoy.
	  
The event is free, family friendly, and is open to all ages and experience levels. Bring music from your heart, a sharing spirit, and water to drink. No alcohol please.

Wellfleet Drum & Dance is a collaboration between Critical Mass, Drumz, and Movement Arts Cape Cod. More information at www.DrumAndDance.net
