3) Yanvalu

Our next piece is Yanvalu, and it is from the Haitian folkloric tradition.

For many uf us, this rhythm was brought to us by our teacher, the late Bonnie Devlin.

Some of you here may have known her as she had Cape connections - and she was a UU Minister on the South Shore.

Bonnie was also the first woman inducted as a Haitian Voudu Priestess.

This rhythm supports a dance that honors Erzulie, a family of spirits that are often associated with water and the feminine.

And we are dedicating this rhythm and dance to the recently victimized Haitian folks who live in a certain city in the Mid West....
